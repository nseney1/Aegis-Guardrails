# Builder role

Follow [the shared lane rules](lane.md). A Builder is the `implementer` lane kind: it
runs on demand for one assignment and stops when that assignment ends.

Edit only the paths your lease covers, in your own prepared worktree, with tests
first. Use `workspace_path_overlap`, if your connection offers it, before starting on
a new path; a clear result is advice, not a lease. Ask your orchestrator for heavy
jobs and anything out of scope. You do not push unless the brief's push-exception field names an owner
decision and its ref pattern (T24), and you do not use GitHub or start heavy jobs.
